01What this is

Nine pages posted to the metzdowd cryptography mailing list on 31 Oct 2008, six weeks after Lehman failed. It defines peer-to-peer electronic cash: double-spending solved by proof-of-work over a public chain rather than a trusted third party, privacy by pseudonymous keys. Implemented weeks later with a fixed 21m issuance cap (in code, not the paper) and a genesis block (3 Jan 2009) embedding the Times headline “Chancellor on brink of second bailout for banks.” It is the only document in this corpus whose author is unverified — text Tier 1, authorship Tier 3.

02Why it matters

Everything downstream — BIS 2018 X2, Lagarde 2018 X3, BIS 2020 X4, and the MAS build from Ubin to PBM P1 — is downstream of the possibility this paper proved: money can move at scale without banks. The official corpus kept the distributed ledger and inverted its properties — permissionless became licensed, bearer became credentialed, irreversible became revocable.

03Key findings

Finding 1 — The starting gun. Crisis (Sep 2008) and counter-idea (Oct 2008) are six weeks apart. Whatever is contested, the sequence is not: the digital-currency era begins as a response to the bailout system, stated in the author's own genesis message.
Finding 2 — Point-by-point inversion. Every Bitcoin parameter is flipped in the PBM/CBDC stack: fixed cap → discretionary issuance; permissionless → designated deployers P4; bearer → identity-bound G7; irreversible → revocable P1; no expiry → configurable expiry; no operator → FMI-grade operator reinstated P4.
Finding 3 — The absorption. Ubin Phase 2 proved the operator could be removed P6; the Blueprint put it back P4. The revolution's technology was retained; its point was not.

05The Money Trail

There is no commercial sponsor in the paper — the money trail is the absence of one, and a ~1m-coin fortune never spent. The Tier-3 question this file holds open: could the paper have come from the same central-banking establishment the rest of the corpus documents, as a controlled test of digital currency on a population? What feeds the suspicion: surgical timing (six weeks post-Lehman), NSA-designed SHA-256, flawless multi-year operational pseudonymity, and a fast official harvest — taxonomy X2, permission X3, doctrine X4, pilots (Ubin). What cuts against it: a documented 25-year cypherpunk lineage (Chaum, Back, Dai, Szabo), a genesis message taunting the bailout system, idiosyncratic early code, and a contemporaneous BIS posture that was hostile (“Bitcoin not the answer,” Mar 2018). Under SA-DOC v1 this stays a question, never a fact. The claim that survives either way is Tier 1: whoever wrote it, Bitcoin became the world's live, unpaid experiment in digital money, and the official corpus harvested the technology while inverting its properties.

06The Gap

The load-bearing gaps. (1) Authorship: unestablished — treated here as a question, not a finding, per Tier-3 discipline. (2) The population-effects contrast: Bitcoin tested on volunteers who chose self-custody, irreversibility and bearer risk; the voucher stack deploys expiry, merchant locks and revocability on enrollees who never opted in and are not shown the clock P1. (3) Ownership of results: the official sector harvested seventeen years of free field data without having to author the experiment.
