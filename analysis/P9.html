01What this is

A tri-central-bank problem-definition report (Bank of Canada, Bank of England, MAS) written with an HSBC-led commercial working group and KPMG as facilitator. It diagnoses the root causes of cross-border friction and sketches three future-state models — the third of which is a wholesale CBDC in three variants: single-currency, multi-currency, and a currency-basket-backed token. The report is explicitly hypothetical and "not intended to pick a model."

02Why it matters

The de-risking datum — an 8% decline in active correspondent banks while flows climb toward US$30tn — is the political-economy engine of the whole cross-border track. Fewer intermediaries carrying more value is concentration risk, and this report reframes it as an innovation opportunity. Its own admission that "incremental changes may need a paradigm shift" is the licence every later experiment draws on.

03Key findings

Finding 1 — De-risking is the burning platform. Volumes rising toward US$30tn while correspondent capacity shrinks 8%: fewer nodes carrying more flow. The central banks are not innovating for novelty; they are hedging a concentrating private utility.
Finding 2 — The basket W-CBDC is the quiet radical. A wholesale CBDC backed by a currency basket — an SDR-adjacent idea — is placed on official trilateral paper here and never publicly pursued. Libra was pilloried for the same shape months later.
Finding 3 — HSBC as scribe. The lead commercial author is the world’s correspondent-banking incumbent; the "future-state capabilities" list reads as what incumbents can live with. The two incremental models — enhance the existing rails — are the ones that got the real-world investment.

05The Money Trail

The report is framed as neutral central-bank problem definition, but its commercial authorship sits with HSBC — the largest correspondent-banking incumbent — alongside a bank working group (OCBC, TD, UOB) whose fee base is precisely the correspondent layer under examination, with KPMG facilitating. The "future-state capabilities" that emerged privilege the incremental models (1 and 2) that incumbents can live with, while the radical basket-W-CBDC was placed on official paper but owned by no one. SWIFT, never named, is the gravitational constant every model is quietly measured against. Disclosure of these commercial positions is partial at best — the incumbent helped write the description of the future it most needs to survive. See the compliance root cause traced onward in Mandala P13 and the plumbing tested in Jasper–Ubin P8.

06The Gap

Three silences. (1) The concentration number: more flow through fewer nodes implies a rising systemic-risk ratio that the report never prints. (2) De-risking’s AML origin: the correspondent decline is regulator-caused — compliance cost — so the central banks propose technology fixes to a problem their own rulebooks created; Mandala P13 addresses that root five years later. (3) No operationalisation: the report decides nothing by design, deferring every hard choice to the experiments it licenses.
