Stage 3 — Research Note

1. Executive Summary

G7 Guardian's foundational framework, co-authored by MAS (Alan Lim lead) and the BIS, defines the archetypes and rules of engagement for tokenised-asset networks: FMI principles apply, regulated FIs serve as trust anchors issuing credentials, and interoperability comes via common infrastructure, a layered global base (GL1's seed), or interlinked networks. It candidly concedes public networks' order-of-magnitude reach advantage, then the programme proceeds permissioned regardless. Its highest immediate value to the practice is biographical: the approach target's published doctrine is here, citable line by line.
